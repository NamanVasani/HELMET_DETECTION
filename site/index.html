Questions

Straight answers about what Helmet does and does not do.

How accurate is it?

It depends on camera angle, distance and light. On its test dataset the model finds most riders, but every site is different, so a pilot starts by measuring accuracy on your own footage. Every case is reviewed by a person before anyone acts.

Does it read number plates?

It suggests a plate and its text where it can. Plate reading is a convenience, not proof: reviewers can correct it, and the plate-to-rider match is a suggestion.

Does it identify people or issue fines?

No. Helmet is a compliance and safety tool. It does not recognise faces, identify people or send anything to authorities.

What hardware do I need?

A recent computer at the site handles one to three cameras. Larger sites can use a Linux server or a small edge box per gate.

Does it need the internet?

No. Detection, storage and the dashboard all run locally. Internet is only used if you turn on email or WhatsApp alerts.
