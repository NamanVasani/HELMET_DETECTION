How it works

Connect

Add camera streams in the dashboard, or upload a recorded clip to try it.

Detect

A vision model checks each rider; a case is saved only after three consecutive agreeing frames.

Review

Your team confirms or rejects each case with the person, plate and context images side by side.

Improve

Reviewed cases become training data, so the model learns your cameras and lighting.

See it in use

A two-minute walkthrough: real detections from a recorded clip, review with keyboard shortcuts, bulk confirmation, analytics and adding a CCTV camera.

What to expect

  • Accuracy depends on camera angle, distance and light. A pilot starts by measuring it on your own footage.
  • Plate reading is a convenience, not proof. Reviewers can correct it, and plate-to-rider matching is a suggestion.
  • Helmet is a compliance and safety tool. It does not identify people or issue fines.
