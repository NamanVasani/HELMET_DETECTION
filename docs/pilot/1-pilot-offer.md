# Helmet pilot — helmet compliance at one gate

**For:** campuses, corporate parks, factories, residential societies and parking operators with two-wheeler entry.

## What Helmet does
- Watches the two-wheeler entry on your existing CCTV (or a small camera we add).
- Flags riders **without a helmet**, with a photo of the rider, the scene and (where readable) the number plate.
- Your team **confirms or rejects every case** in one tap. Nothing is treated as a violation until a person confirms it.
- Sends alerts (WhatsApp, email) and a daily PDF summary.
- Runs on **one computer at your site**. Footage does not go to the cloud.

## What we need from you
1. Permission to install at one gate, and a person who can help us on install day.
2. A computer at the site (Mac, Windows or Linux; recent, with 8 GB RAM), or space for our mini PC.
3. Access to the camera stream (RTSP address from your CCTV recorder), or permission to mount a camera.
4. A notice board at the gate telling riders that helmet checks are recorded (we provide the text).

## How we measure success
At the start we agree three numbers, and we report them at the end:
1. **Detection rate:** of riders without a helmet that a person sees on the footage, how many Helmet flagged.
2. **False-alarm rate:** of cases Helmet flagged, how many your team marked *false positive*.
3. **Time saved:** minutes per day your team spends on helmet checks, before and after.

We measure accuracy on **your** footage in week 1, so you know what to expect before relying on it.

## Privacy
- Riders wearing a helmet have their faces blurred and their plates discarded.
- Unconfirmed evidence is deleted automatically after 30 days.
- Every sign-in, review and export is logged. Data stays on your premises during and after the pilot.
- Helmet does not identify people, recognise faces or contact authorities.

## After the pilot
You decide: continue, extend to more gates, or stop. If you stop, we remove the software and hand over or delete the data, as you choose.
