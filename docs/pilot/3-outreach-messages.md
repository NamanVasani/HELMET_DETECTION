# Outreach messages

Short, honest messages to start a pilot conversation. Replace the [brackets]. Send to the person who runs security, admin or facilities.

## WhatsApp (first message)
> Hi [Name], I'm [Your name]. I've built **Helmet**, a small system that checks whether two-wheeler riders at a gate are wearing a helmet, using the CCTV you already have. It flags riders without a helmet with a photo for your team to confirm, sends a daily summary, and keeps all footage on your premises.
>
> Would you be open to a short pilot at one gate of [Organisation]? Setup takes about 30 minutes. I can show a 2-minute demo first. [Demo link]

## WhatsApp (follow-up after 3–4 days)
> Hi [Name], following up on Helmet. Happy to come by for 15 minutes and show it on one of your camera feeds, no commitment. Would [day] or [day] work?

## Email
**Subject:** Helmet compliance at your gate — pilot proposal

> Dear [Name],
>
> I'm [Your name], the developer of **Helmet**, a system that checks helmet use by two-wheeler riders at entry gates using existing CCTV cameras.
>
> **How it works:** Helmet flags riders without a helmet and shows your team the rider, the scene and the number plate where readable. Your team confirms or rejects each case in one tap; nothing counts as a violation until a person confirms it. You get WhatsApp or email alerts and a daily PDF report. All footage stays on a computer at your site.
>
> **Pilot proposal:** one gate, [length], [price]. In the first week we measure accuracy on your own footage and share the results with you.
>
> I've attached a one-page summary. Could we set up a 15-minute call or visit next week?
>
> Regards,
> [Your name] · [Phone] · [Email]

## Call / visit talking points
1. Ask first: how do they check helmets today? Who does it? How long does it take? What happens to violators?
2. Show the demo: Quick review, a daily report, an alert on a phone.
3. Be clear about limits: accuracy depends on camera angle and light, plates are a hint not proof, and Helmet does not identify people.
4. Agree the three pilot numbers (detection rate, false-alarm rate, time saved).
5. Next step: a date for install day, and who will be the contact on site.
